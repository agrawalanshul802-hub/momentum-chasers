❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions About Our Platform

Have questions before getting started? Here are direct, transparent answers about Momentum Chasers.

What is Momentum Chasers and how does it help my trading? +
Momentum Chasers is India's premier quantitative market screener and terminal. It aggregates market quotes across 2,000+ NSE and BSE equities, computes mathematical indicators (10/20/50/200 EMAs, RSI, VWAP), tracks 15-minute derivatives order flow, and monitors sector relative turnover. It helps you identify high-probability swing and momentum setups in seconds without opening 20 different browser tabs.
Is Momentum Chasers free to use? +
Yes! Retail traders can sign in with Google or Email to explore our full quantitative analytics suite, smart screener, market breadth radar, sector heatmap, and sync starred watchlists across devices.
Do I need to link a live broker account to use the platform? +
No broker login is required to use the screening tools, order flow, or market breadth! If you wish to use our Multi-Demat Portfolio Tracker, you can easily upload standard statement exports (CSV/Excel) from Zerodha Console, Groww, Angel One, or Dhan. Your data is stored safely in your browser local storage.
Which stock exchanges and universes are covered? +
Momentum Chasers covers the entire Indian equity landscape across both the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE). This includes Nifty 50, Nifty Next 50, Nifty Midcap 150, Nifty Smallcap 250, all 200+ F&O traded stock futures, 44 sectoral benchmarks, and a curated universe of 250+ IPOs listed between 2024 and 2026.
Is Momentum Chasers a SEBI registered investment adviser? +
No. Momentum Chasers is strictly a financial technology, quantitative screening, and mathematical education platform. We do not provide buy/sell tips, portfolio management services, or individual investment advice. All trades you execute are self-directed. Please read our Terms & Conditions and consult a SEBI registered financial adviser before trading in equity derivatives.
