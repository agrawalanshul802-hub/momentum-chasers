Questions before you start

Short answers to what most learners ask first.

Do I need trading experience?

No. The first courses start from zero and explain every term. Later courses are for people who already place trades.

Will you tell me what to buy or sell?

No. These are learning courses. They teach you how to read the market and manage risk so you can make your own decisions.

How do I sign in?

Use your Google account, or sign in with your email and password. If you forget the password, choose Forgot password and we email you a reset link.

Can I learn on my phone?

Yes. The site works in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, so there is nothing to install.

How long does it take to finish?

It is self-paced, so it depends on how often you study. Most people work through one short lesson at a time, a few times a week.

Do I need a trading account to learn?

No. You can learn the ideas and practise on sample charts without opening an account or risking money.

What market tools are included?

There are 14 tools. Core tools: Smart Screener, Sector Indices, Sector Turnover, Market Mood (MMI), A/D Net Flow and AI Stock Analysis. Market intel and demat: Events Calendar, Bulk / Block Deals, Market News, Market Heatmap, Market Valuation and Multi-Demat Tracker. Mainboard IPO info: IPO Timetable and IPO Performance Tracker. You can also star stocks to keep a watchlist.

Which markets do the examples use?

The examples focus on Indian stocks and index derivatives, so the charts and terms look familiar if you trade in India.
