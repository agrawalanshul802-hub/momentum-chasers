User Data Protection Standards

Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026 • Compliance: Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023

01 Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Momentum Chasers, we believe trader privacy is paramount. We do not sell your personal data, we do not monetize your browsing habits with third-party tracking brokers, and we collect only the minimal data strictly required to authenticate you and preserve your personalized market terminal settings.

02 Information We Collect

When you interact with our platform, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Authentication Information: Your email address and optional full name provided during email signup or Google OAuth sign-in.
  • Terminal Preferences: Your starred stock watchlists, customized screener filters, dark mode layout options, and notification settings (saved in local storage and Supabase).
  • Feedback & Correspondence: When you submit direct feedback, message inquiries, or contact us at support@momentumchasers.in, we collect your provided name, email, rating, and message content to respond to you.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Standard web server logs including IP addresses, browser types, referring pages, and access timestamps for DDoS mitigation and server health monitoring.

03 How We Use Your Information

We process your data exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Authenticating your account via Supabase Auth and maintaining session security.
  • Syncing watchlists and screening presets across your devices (desktop and mobile).
  • Delivering prompt email responses to user bug reports, feedback, and technical inquiries sent to support@momentumchasers.in.
  • Optimizing high-throughput market data caching and quant engine stability.

04 Zero Data Selling & Third-Party Services

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data with trusted infrastructure providers under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Supabase: For cloud database persistence and encrypted user authentication (Row-Level Security enabled).
  • Google Identity Services: Facilitating secure 1-click Sign in with Google (OAuth 2.0).
  • Render Cloud: Hosting the Node.js backend infrastructure and WebSockets live proxy.

05 Data Security & Encryption

We employ defense-in-depth security measures to protect your information:

  • All transmissions between your web browser and our servers are encrypted via 256-bit Transport Layer Security (TLS / SSL HTTPS).
  • User passwords are never stored in plaintext — they are cryptographically salted and hashed using industry-standard bcrypt algorithms managed by Supabase Auth.
  • Access to backend servers and databases is restricted by multi-factor authentication and strict IP whitelisting.

06 Cookies & Local Storage

Momentum Chasers does not use intrusive third-party cross-site advertising cookies. We use standard HTTP session tokens and HTML5 browser Local Storage strictly for:

  • Preserving your active login session token so you stay logged in.
  • Remembering your screener columns, chart layout, and favorite stocks offline.

07 Your Data Rights & Deletion Requests

In full accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023) and global standards, you have the right to:

  • Access and review all personal data stored in your account profile.
  • Request immediate and irreversible deletion of your account and all associated watchlists.
  • To request complete data deletion, simply send an email from your registered address to support@momentumchasers.in with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Your request will be fulfilled within 48 business hours.
