User Data Protection Standards

Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: January 1, 2027 • Last Updated: November 2026 • Operator: Momentum Chasers (Individual Proprietorship) • Compliance: DPDP Act, 2023 (India)

01 Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Momentum Chasers, we believe that trader privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use our Platform at momentumchasers.in and momentum-chasers.onrender.com, how we use it, and your rights over it.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. We collect only the minimum data strictly required to authenticate you, provide the Platform's services, and improve your experience.

02 Information We Collect

When you interact with our Platform, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Authentication Data: Your email address and optional display name provided during Email/Password registration or Google OAuth sign-in. For Google sign-in, we receive only your name, email address, and profile picture URL — no access to your Google Drive, Gmail, or other Google services.
  • Portfolio & Watchlist Data: Stock holdings, purchase prices, quantities, and watchlisted symbols that you voluntarily enter into the Portfolio Tracker and Watchlist Manager. This data is stored in Supabase and is accessible only by you.
  • Screener Preferences: Your customized screener column configurations, sort orders, filters, and UI layout preferences (stored in browser localStorage).
  • VIP Subscription Records: When you purchase a VIP subscription, we maintain a record of your registered email, UPI transaction ID provided by you, subscription plan, and activation date. We do not store your UPI ID or bank account details.
  • Feedback & Correspondence: If you submit the in-app feedback form or email support@momentumchasers.in, we collect your name, email, star rating, and message content to respond to you.
  • Waitlist / Notify Me: If you submit your email through the Coming Soon page (Formspree-powered form), your email is processed by Formspree and stored for pre-launch notification purposes only.
  • Technical Server Logs: Standard web server logs including IP addresses, browser user-agent, referring pages, accessed URLs, and timestamps — used for DDoS mitigation, server health monitoring, and debugging.

03 How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data only for the following legitimate operational purposes:

  • Authenticating your account via Supabase Auth (Email/Password or Google OAuth) and maintaining secure session tokens.
  • Syncing your stock watchlists, portfolio holdings, and screener preferences across your devices in real-time via Supabase.
  • Activating and verifying your VIP subscription access after confirmed UPI payment.
  • Responding to your feedback, bug reports, refund requests, and support queries via support@momentumchasers.in.
  • Sending pre-launch notifications to users who signed up on the Coming Soon waitlist (opt-out available on request).
  • Optimizing server-side market data caching pipelines and diagnosing technical issues via anonymized server logs.

04 Third-Party Services & Data Processors

To deliver the Platform, we rely on the following trusted third-party service providers. Each is subject to their own privacy policy:

  • Supabase (supabase.co) — Cloud PostgreSQL database, Row-Level Security (RLS) enforced user auth, encrypted storage of portfolio/watchlist data. Supabase Privacy Policy ↗
  • Google Identity Services (OAuth 2.0) — Secure "Sign in with Google" authentication. Only your name, email, and profile photo are shared with our Platform. Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • Render.com — Cloud hosting of the Node.js backend server and WebSocket live data proxy. Server logs may be retained by Render per their policy. Render Privacy Policy ↗
  • TradingView — Embedded charting widgets on Stock Deep Dive pages. TradingView may set their own cookies per their privacy policy. TradingView Privacy Policy ↗
  • Yahoo Finance API — Market data (stock prices, historical OHLC, 52W High/Low). No personal user data is shared with Yahoo Finance.
  • Formspree.io — Processes Coming Soon page email collection forms. Formspree Privacy Policy ↗
  • Google Fonts (CDN) — Typography fonts loaded from Google's CDN. Google may log your IP for font delivery. Google Fonts Info ↗

We do not engage any advertising networks, data brokers, or behavioral tracking services. No personal data is shared with any party outside the above listed processors.

05 Market Data We Process (Non-Personal)

The following market data is processed server-side to power the Platform's features. This is public financial data, not personal data:

  • NSE/BSE Stock Universe (~2,709 symbols) — ticker symbols, company names, sector classifications.
  • Live Prices & OHLCV — Fetched from Yahoo Finance API for real-time screener updates.
  • FII/DII Historical Data — Sourced from NSE's official public disclosures, cached in our server.
  • Sector EMA & Breadth Data — Computed internally via our daily market pipeline scripts.
  • Market Valuation Metrics — Nifty P/E, P/B, Dividend Yield sourced from public NSE data.
  • Market Mood Index (MMI) — Derived sentiment indicator computed from multiple breadth inputs.
  • IPO PDF Notes — Curated analytical documents authored and hosted by the Platform operator.
  • Trading Ideas — Proprietary swing/positional ideas published by the operator (not personal data).

06 Cookies & Browser Storage

Momentum Chasers uses only functional cookies and browser storage — zero advertising or tracking cookies:

  • Supabase Auth Token (localStorage) — Keeps you logged in across browser sessions. Cleared on logout.
  • Screener Layout Preferences (localStorage) — Saves your column visibility, sort preferences, and filter states offline.
  • Watchlist & Portfolio Cache (localStorage) — Temporarily caches your data for faster offline loading.
  • Owner Preview Cookie (mc_owner_preview) — A short-lived session cookie enabling the Platform operator to bypass the Coming Soon page on momentumchasers.in for internal testing. This cookie is not set for general users.
  • TradingView Cookies — TradingView's embedded chart widget may set its own functional cookies as per TradingView's cookie policy.

07 Data Retention

  • Account Data (Supabase): Retained for the lifetime of your active account. Permanently deleted within 48 business hours upon verified deletion request.
  • Server Logs: Retained for up to 30 days for security and debugging purposes, then automatically purged.
  • Feedback Messages: Retained as long as necessary to respond and improve the Platform. Can be deleted on request.
  • VIP Subscription Records: Retained for accounting and dispute resolution purposes for a minimum of 2 years as required by Indian law.
  • Waitlist Emails (Formspree): Retained until the Platform launches publicly or you request removal.

08 Data Security & Encryption

We employ defense-in-depth security measures to protect your personal information:

  • All data transmissions between your browser and our servers use 256-bit TLS/SSL HTTPS encryption.
  • Passwords are never stored in plaintext — they are cryptographically hashed using industry-standard bcrypt algorithms managed by Supabase Auth.
  • Supabase database enforces Row-Level Security (RLS) — your data rows are readable and writable only by your authenticated session, not by other users or public requests.
  • Backend server access is protected by API key authentication and environment variable secrets stored securely on Render.

09 Your Rights Under DPDP Act, 2023

In accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, you have the following rights as a data principal:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data. Send a deletion request email to support@momentumchasers.in with subject "Data Deletion Request". We will fulfill your request within 48 business hours.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent for data processing at any time by deleting your account. Note that withdrawal of consent may result in inability to use authenticated features of the Platform.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You may contact our Data Grievance Officer at support@momentumchasers.in for any privacy-related concerns. We endeavour to respond within 7 working days.

10 Children's Privacy

Momentum Chasers is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18 years. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe that a minor has registered on our Platform, please contact us at support@momentumchasers.in and we will promptly delete the account and associated data.
